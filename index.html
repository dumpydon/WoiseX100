Terms of use

WoiseX100 is an interview demo built for the 100x assessment. You can explore it by asking questions by voice or text.

Live replies are generated by AI and can contain mistakes. Verify any details you plan to rely on.

Privacy Policy

WoiseX100 keeps the visible transcript in this tab’s memory. Refresh or start a new conversation to clear it.

Live replies send your question and recent messages to OpenAI for processing.

Opening Voice mode requests microphone access and starts listening. Stop the mic or close voice mode to stop capture. Depending on your browser, dictation may send audio to its speech recognition service. You can use text at any time.

Your interface-sounds preference is saved on this device. It does not save your conversations.
